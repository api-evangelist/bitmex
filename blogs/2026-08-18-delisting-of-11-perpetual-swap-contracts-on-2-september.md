On 2 September 2026 at 12:00 UTC, we will delist 11 perpetual swap contracts (please see the list below). The contracts will be settled early at 12:00 UTC on 2 September 2026 ( “ T settle ”), according to the standard practice described in our Exchange Guide . The decision to delist these contracts has been made due to the decision to close the BitMEX exchange.
