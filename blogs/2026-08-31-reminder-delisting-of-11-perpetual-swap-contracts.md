A reminder that on 2 September 2026 at 12:00 UTC, BitMEX will delist 11 perpetual swap contracts. The contracts will be settled early at 12:00 UTC on 2 September 2026 ( “ T settle ”), according to the standard practice described in our Exchange Guide . The decision to delist these contracts has been made due to insufficient trading interest and the decision to close the BitMEX exchange.
