On 16 September 2026 at 12:00 UTC, BitMEX will delist and settle the XBTUSD, XBTUSDT, ETHUSD and ETHUSDT Perpetual Swap contracts and the XBTU26, XBTZ26, XBTH27 and ETHUSDU26 Futures contracts. This comes following our announcement on the closure of the BitMEX exchange on 23 September 2026 (more details here ). The contract will be settled early according to the standard practice described in our Exchange Guide .
