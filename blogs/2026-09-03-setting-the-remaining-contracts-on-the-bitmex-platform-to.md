On 7 September 2026 at 04:00 UTC, BitMEX will set all remaining Futures contracts to Reduce Only mode. This impacts ETHUSDU26, XBTU26, XBTZ26 and XBTH27. On 8 September 2026 at 04:00 UTC, BitMEX will set ETHUSD and ETHUSDT to Reduce Only mode.
